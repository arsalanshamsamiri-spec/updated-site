Competition history

Team 2141 has competed in 20 seasons since its rookie year in 2007, at 23 events in Northern California and one in San Diego.

Over the years

2007 to 2009

Our rookie season was 2007 at the Silicon Valley Regional in San Jose. In 2008 we went to two regionals, San Diego and Silicon Valley. San Diego is still the only event we've attended outside Northern California. ABC7 News covered the 2008 Silicon Valley Regional and included the team in its report.

2010 to 2013

We moved to the Sacramento Regional in Davis in 2010, ranked 7th and reached the quarterfinals. In 2011 we ranked last of 53 at Sacramento, then 4th at Silicon Valley a few weeks later, which is still our best qualification rank. We ranked 10th at Sacramento in 2013.

2016 to 2018

We reached the Sacramento semifinals three years in a row. In 2016 we were the second pick for Alliance 6. In 2017 we ranked 9th of 56 with a 6–2–1 record and captained Alliance 6, picking 1072 Harker Robotics and 5480. In 2018 we were the first pick for Alliance 5, which scored 407 points in a quarterfinal, the highest score in team history.

2019 to 2025

2019 was a 3–6 season. The 2020 Sacramento Regional was cancelled because of COVID-19, and there were no in-person events in 2021. We came back at Silicon Valley in 2022 with a 3–5–1 record and improved after that: 5–5 in 2023, 6–6 in 2024 and 5–4 at the East Bay Regional in 2025.

2026

California's new district system meant two events instead of one. We went 5–7 at both, and at Contra Costa we were picked for Alliance 3, which finished fourth.
