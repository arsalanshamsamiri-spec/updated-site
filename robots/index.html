Robots

Every January, FIRST announces a new game and teams have a few weeks to build a robot for it. These are our robots from the last three seasons, followed by the two robot names that appear in older records.

2026 Rebuilt

Played 24 qualification matches across two district events. Its best match was 315 points at the Sacramento District Event. At Contra Costa it was the second-round pick for Alliance 3, which finished fourth.

2025 Reefscape

Placed coral on the reef and went 5–4 in qualification matches at the East Bay Regional. That was our first winning qualification record since 2018.

2024 Crescendo

Shot foam rings into the speaker and amp, and went 6–6 at the Sacramento Regional, including four straight wins in the middle of the event.

Leonidas 2015 to 2023

The robot name on our registration for nine seasons, after the Spartan king. Under this name we reached the Sacramento semifinals in 2016, 2017 and 2018. In 2017 we ranked 9th of 56 and captained Alliance 6.

Sparticus Early years

One of our first robot names. It appears on an early Silicon Valley Regional team list, with De La Salle High School and AT&T West as sponsors.

Software

Our robots are programmed in Java with WPILib, the standard FRC library, in Visual Studio Code. The code is public on GitHub under the MIT license, and other teams are welcome to use it.

The Spartonics-Code repository has the 2017, 2018 and 2019 robot code, a BattleBot side project, and a getting-started guide for new programmers.
