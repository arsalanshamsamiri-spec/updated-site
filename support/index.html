Support the team

Spartronics is funded by De La Salle High School and by sponsors. The money pays for event registration, robot parts and tools, and travel to competitions.

Email us about sponsoring

Sponsorship

Companies that sponsor FRC teams usually have their logo on the robot and the team shirts. Email us and a student from the business sub-team will get back to you with details. You're welcome to visit the shop and see the robot.

Our sponsors

  • De La Salle High School, every season since 2007
  • Nvidia, 2016 to 2023
  • AT&T West, in the team's first seasons

Other ways to help

Machining, materials and time are as useful to us as money. If you're an engineer, machinist or programmer and could spend some time in the shop, we'd like to hear from you.

To make a donation, email us and we'll send instructions.
