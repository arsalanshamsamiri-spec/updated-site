About Spartronics

Spartronics is FIRST Robotics Competition Team 2141. We're based at De La Salle High School in Concord, and our members come from De La Salle and from Carondelet High School, its sister school next door.

The name

De La Salle's teams are the Spartans, and the robotics team took its name from that. For most of its history the team was registered as Spartonics. Since 2024 the official FIRST records have spelled it Spartronics, and that's the name we use now. Our GitHub organization still has the original spelling.

Our robots have carried Spartan names as well: Sparticus in the early years and Leonidas from 2015 to 2023.

How the team works

Students do the design, building, wiring, programming and business work. Mentors teach skills and supervise the shop. Each member joins one of five sub-teams: mechanical, design, electrical, programming or business. The Join page describes what each one does.

We work out of De La Salle's Fabrication Room, a shop the school shares between robotics, technical theatre, sculpture and engineering classes. The school also offers a for-credit course called Robotics, Engineering, and Programming, which is where many members first try the work.

About FIRST

FIRST is a nonprofit founded in 1989 by the inventor Dean Kamen. In the FIRST Robotics Competition, high school teams get a new game every January and have a few weeks to build a robot for it, then compete at events through the spring. Teams are expected to help each other along the way, which FIRST calls Gracious Professionalism.

For younger students, FIRST also runs FIRST LEGO League for elementary and middle school and FIRST Tech Challenge for grades 7 through 12. You can find a program near you at firstinspires.org.
