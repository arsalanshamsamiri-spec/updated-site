Join the team

Any De La Salle or Carondelet student can join, in any grade. You don't need experience. Most members learn the shop tools, CAD or Java after they start.

Email us about joining

Meetings

The team meets in the Fabrication Room at De La Salle. From the game announcement in early January until our last event in the spring, we also meet on some evenings and Saturdays.

The easiest way to start is to come by the Fabrication Room during a meeting, or email us and we'll tell you when the next one is.

Sub-teams

Mechanical
Builds the robot. Mechanical cuts, drills and assembles parts in the Fabrication Room and repairs the robot between matches at events.
Design
Models the robot in CAD before anything is built, and makes the drawings and 3D-printed parts that mechanical works from.
Electrical
Wires the control system, motors, sensors and pneumatics, and keeps the wiring working through a full event.
Programming
Writes the robot code in Java, including the driver controls and the autonomous routine that runs at the start of each match. Our code is public on GitHub.
Business
Finds sponsors, tracks the budget, runs the team's social media and writes award submissions for competitions.

Questions

Do I need to know how to build or code?

No. New members learn from older students and mentors in the fall, before the game is announced.

Can Carondelet students join?

Yes. The team is open to students from both schools.

When is the busy part of the year?

From the game announcement in early January through our last event. In 2026 our events were in March and April. Expect more meetings during those months.

What do parents need to know?

Every student needs a FIRST consent and release form on file before competing. Events are weekend competitions in Northern California, usually Friday through Sunday. Parents who want to help with transport, food or the pit area are welcome.
