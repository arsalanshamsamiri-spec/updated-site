The robotics team at De La Salle and Carondelet

Spartronics is FIRST Robotics Competition Team 2141 from Concord, California. Students have designed, built and programmed a new competition robot every season since 2007.

Join the team See our competition history

The 2026 season

California moved to a district system in 2026, so instead of one large regional we played two smaller events. We went 5–7 at the Sacramento District Event in Elk Grove, with our best match of the year at 315 points.

Two weeks later at the Contra Costa District Event in Pittsburg, Alliance 3 picked us in the second round to play alongside 6619 and 2135 Presentation Invasion. The alliance won its first two playoff matches and finished fourth. It was our first time in the playoffs since 2018.

The 2027 game, BIOCORE, is announced in January. We're registered for the Contra Costa District Event on April 2–4, 2027.
